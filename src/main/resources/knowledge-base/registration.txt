Patient Registration Process:
1. Online: Visit hospital website or mobile app, click "Register", fill form, get patient ID
2. Walk-in: Go to Registration Desk (Building A, Ground Floor), bring ID and insurance card
3. Required documents: Photo ID, insurance card, referral (if required), medication list
4. New patients: Arrive 30 minutes early for paperwork
5. Returning patients: Check-in at kiosk or front desk

Appointment and Queue System:
- Self-registration: Patients enter symptoms, AI recommends department
- Queue types: Emergency (immediate), Appointment (scheduled time), Walk-in (FIFO)
- Queue numbers: Format Q{DEPT}{YYYYMMDD}{SEQ} (e.g., QCAR202608240001)
- Track queue: Mobile app, waiting room displays, SMS notifications
- Cancellation: Free up to 2 hours before, late cancellation may incur fee
